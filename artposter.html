This poster was designed for the 'Event' theme of the project, specifically for the art exhibition 'Möten av Färg och Form' (Meetings of Color and Form).

Inspired by Neville Brody's work with vibrant colors, the poster aims to inspire the viewer with a sense of innovation and creativity. The design employs a playful interaction of geometric shapes and a lively color palette to capture the artistic essence of the exhibition.

The typeface used for both the heading and body text is Futura, with varying weights and sizes to establish hierarchy and guide the viewer.
