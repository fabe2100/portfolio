Bookstore Business Cards

  • Business Cards
  • Affinity Designer
  • Typography
  • Brand Identity

This project involved designing business cards, containing complete contact details including title, name, postal address, phone, and email. Typography choices ensure readability and clarity.

The design reflects the bookstore theme with a simple color palette of dusty rose background and bookish brown text and logo, emphasizing the content and evoking a classic, literary feel. The Garamond font was chosen for its classic style fitting the bookstore concept.

Typography hierarchy was carefully considered to highlight important information such as name and title, with smaller text for contact details accompanied by icons for clarity.
